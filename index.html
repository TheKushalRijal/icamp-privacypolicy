This policy explains how the Icamp mobile app for iOS and Android ("Icamp", "we", "us") handles your information. Icamp helps international students in the United States find university information, housing, documents, study materials and campus photos.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and name. These come from email sign-up, or from Google or Apple if you use Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple. If you sign up with email, your password is stored only in hashed (unreadable) form. If you choose Apple's Hide My Email, we receive a private relay address instead of your real email.
  • Your university: the university you select, so the app can show content for it.
  • Content you upload: photos and study materials (such as notes or PDFs), with the captions, titles, course and class details you add.
  • Reports: when you report a photo or file, we store the report and the reason you picked.
  • Messages to us: if you email us, we keep the email so we can reply.

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifier and platform: a random app-specific ID for your device and whether it runs iOS or Android. We use it to keep you signed in, and to stop banned users from creating new accounts.
  • Basic server logs: like most online services, our servers record technical details of requests (such as time and IP address) for security and troubleshooting. These logs are kept only for a short time.

Permissions

Icamp asks for access to your photo library or camera only when you choose to upload a photo. We only receive the photo you pick or take.

2. What we don't collect

  • No precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, health or financial information.
  • No advertising, advertising IDs or third-party analytics trackers.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no selling or renting of your data.

3. How we use information

  • To create and run your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show content for your university, and to show your uploads to other students with your name.
  • To review reports, remove content that breaks our rules, and prevent abuse, including banning accounts and devices.
  • To answer your questions and support requests.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to comply with the law.

4. Content other students can see

Photos and study materials you upload are shared with other Icamp users at your university, together with your display name. Don't upload anything you don't want others to see.

Anyone can report content they think breaks our rules. Reported content is hidden from everyone right away and reviewed within 7 days. We then either restore it or remove it, and we may ban users who post objectionable content.

5. Service providers

We don't sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own use. We use these providers to run Icamp, and they process data only on our behalf:

  • Amazon Web Services and Supabase: app servers and database hosting.
  • Cloudflare (R2 storage): stores uploaded photos and files.
  • Google / Firebase: Sign in with Google (only if you use it), and a Google Sheets log of uploaded files. That log contains internal IDs and file references only, not names or emails.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple (only if you use it).

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or others.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists.

Deleting your account: in the app, tap the profile icon on the home screen, then choose Delete account. This permanently removes your email address, name, password and device links, and signs you out.

Photos and study materials you shared are not deleted automatically, because other students may rely on them. They stay in the app, shown as an anonymous user and no longer linked to you. To have them removed as well, delete them in the app before deleting your account, or email us.

Report records and moderation decisions (such as bans) may be kept after an account is deleted, to prevent abuse.

7. Your choices and rights

You can see your account details on the Account page in the app, delete your own uploads, and delete your account at any time. To access, correct or delete your information, or to ask a privacy question, email thekushalrijal@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and access to our systems is restricted. No service can guarantee perfect security, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

Icamp is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
